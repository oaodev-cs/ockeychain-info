OC-Keychain Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

In short. OC-Keychain keeps your settings on your device. It does not collect your browsing history or the content of the pages you visit. The only information that leaves your device is your license key when it is verified, and files you choose to download for a skin.

What the Extension accesses. The Extension runs on the web pages you visit so it can draw the cursor and look for skin links or tags on the page. This happens inside your browser. Page content and web addresses are not sent to me.

What is stored on your device. Your settings, images you upload, settings you create for particular sites, and your license status and key are stored in your browser's extension storage on your device. Uninstalling the Extension removes them.

License verification. When you activate the extension, the license key you enter is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a server I operate, hosted with a third-party cloud provider, so that I can confirm the key is valid. As with any online service, the server also receives routine connection information, such as your IP address and the time of the request. I use this information only to check licenses, keep the service running and prevent misuse.

Skins. The Extension downloads a skin only after you choose to apply it. It downloads from the host shown in the prompt. That host can see your internet address and that you requested the files. I do not receive this information.

Payments. Purchases are handled by LemonSqueezy. Its privacy policy applies. I do not receive your full payment card details.

What I do not do. The Extension does not include advertising, analytics or tracking code. I do not sell your data. I do not use or share data for anything unrelated to the Extension's purpose of customizing your cursor.

Children. The Extension is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child gave me information, contact me and I will delete it.

Your choices and rights. You can uninstall the Extension at any time. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU and UK), you may have the right to ask what I hold about you and to ask me to correct or delete it. Email: oaodevcs@yahoo.com.

Changes. I will post changes here with a new effective date.

Contact: oaodevcs@yahoo.com
